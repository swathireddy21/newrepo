About Vahaan Logistics

Built for India's Tech Workforce

Founded in Hyderabad, Vahaan Logistics was built with one mission — to make employee commuting effortless, safe, and transparent for India's booming tech sector.

Today, we serve over 120 software companies across 12 cities, managing thousands of daily commutes with trained drivers, smart technology, and dedicated operations teams.

Safety FirstAll drivers undergo police verification and vehicle fitness checks every 60 days.
Technology-DrivenProprietary roster management, live tracking, and employee self-service app built in-house.
